# CollabSpace Legal / Privacy Implementation Requirements

## Important

These are implementation requirements and drafting guidance, not a claim that CollabSpace is legally compliant.

Final legal text must use the real business identity, jurisdiction, contact details, billing model, data processors, retention periods, and actual product behavior.

Never invent legal/business details.

## Privacy Policy

Must explain, in plain language:

- who operates CollabSpace
- what data is collected
- why it is collected
- legal basis where applicable
- company/tenant membership
- login approval data
- authentication/security logs
- files/documents
- notifications
- contacts
- cookies
- analytics
- third-party services
- retention
- deletion/access/correction processes
- security practices
- contact method
- applicable rights

Data minimization is mandatory.

## Terms and Conditions

Must accurately describe:

- service
- user accounts
- company administration
- acceptable use
- security responsibilities
- intellectual property
- uploaded content
- account suspension/termination
- service availability
- limitations
- liability provisions as legally reviewed
- governing law/jurisdiction as applicable
- contact information

Do not add unenforceable or invented claims.

## Cookie Policy

Maintain an inventory of actual cookies/storage technologies.

For each:

- name
- provider
- purpose
- type
- duration
- required/optional classification

Do not document cookies that are not actually used.

Do not claim that a cookie banner is required or unnecessary without auditing actual technologies and applicable jurisdiction.

## Refund Policy

Must match the actual commercial model.

If the service is free, do not pretend a paid refund process exists.

If paid subscriptions exist, document:

- billing cycle
- cancellation
- refund eligibility
- applicable statutory consumer rights
- contact process

## Form Consent

Forms must:

- collect only necessary fields
- explain purpose
- link to Privacy Policy where appropriate
- separate optional marketing consent from required operational processing
- never use pre-ticked optional consent
- provide accessible labels and error messages

## File Privacy

Uploaded company files are private business data.

The privacy/security design must state that access is authorization-controlled.

Do not promise absolute protection against screenshots or external capture.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Check:

- keyboard navigation
- focus indicators
- labels
- error messages
- contrast
- alt text
- headings
- semantic controls
- screen-reader compatibility

## Content Integrity

Remove:

- fake reviews
- fake user counts
- fake logos
- fake awards
- unsupported certifications
- unsupported security/compliance claims
- placeholder legal/business details

## Legal Implementation Gate

Before release:

- verify actual company/legal identity
- verify privacy contact
- verify actual cookies/analytics
- verify third-party processors
- verify actual billing/refund model
- verify file handling
- verify forms
- verify deployed behavior against legal pages
